# Here to Slay Digital

A digital version of the *Here to Slay* card game for friends playing together, in person or online.

## Multiplayer

**Room**:
One game session and the players seated in it, from the lobby through game over.
_Avoid_: Lobby (the pre-game screen only), match, session

**Host**:
The player whose device runs the Room and holds the full, unredacted game state. The Host is also an ordinary player.
_Avoid_: Server, owner, admin

**Room Code**:
The short code other players enter to join a Room.
_Avoid_: Invite ID, game ID

**Seat**:
A position at the table in a Room, filled by a local player on the Host's device, a remote player, or a bot. A Room may mix all three.
_Avoid_: Slot, player slot
